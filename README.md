## ✨ Features

- 🎯 **4 Documentary Categories** — Wildlife, Indigenous Culture, Earth & Geography, Cities & Civilization
- 📚 **Preset Library** — Pre-loaded subjects like Komodo Dragon, Orangutan, Suku Baduy, Raja Ampat, and more
- 🎙️ **Teleprompter-Ready Scripts** — Natural, flowing narration in Indonesian or English (BBC/National Geographic style)
- 📜 **12-Segment Story Structure** — Professional documentary arc from Cold Opening to Poetic Closing
- 🔬 **Master Visual Consistency Sheet** — Unified visual identity for AI image/video generation
- 🎬 **Shot-by-Shot Storyboard** — Complete with camera specs, composition, movement, and dual AI prompts (T2I + I2V)
- 🎵 **Music & SFX Mapping** — Orchestral tracks and sound effects per segment
- ⏱️ **Duration Flexibility** — 3, 5, 8, 10, or 15 minutes
- 🌓 **Light/Dark Theme** — Comfortable viewing in any environment
- 📦 **Multi-Format Export** — TXT, Markdown, DOC, and JSON
- 🔌 **Multi-API Support** — OpenRouter (free models) & Google Gemini
